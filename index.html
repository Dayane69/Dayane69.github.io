autok

autok is a personal, non-commercial tool used by a single creator to produce short educational videos in French and to schedule them on their own social media accounts.

What it does

  • Turns a short topic description into a fact-checked video script.
  • Produces a vertical video (voice-over, subtitles, illustrations) on the creator's own computer.
  • After the creator has reviewed and approved the video, uploads it to the creator's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API, at the time the creator chose.

autok is not offered to the public. Only the creator's own accounts are connected to it.
