Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Who we are

autok is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by Dayane. It has no users other than the operator.

Data we access

When the operator connects their own TikTok account, the app receives, with the operator's consent:

  • basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) to identify the connected account;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, used only to upload and publish videos on that account;
  • the list and status of videos published through the app, to confirm publication.

When the operator connects their own YouTube channel through Google OAuth, the app receives, with the operator's consent, the channel's basic information, OAuth tokens used only to upload the operator's approved videos, and the statistics of those videos (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API).

autok's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How data is stored and used

All data is stored on the operator's own computer. Tokens are stored encrypted. Data is used only to publish the operator's own videos and is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data about other TikTok or YouTube users is collected.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account is connected. Disconnecting the account in the app, or revoking access in TikTok's or Google's account settings, removes the app's access; stored tokens are then deleted.

Politique de confidentialité (français)
