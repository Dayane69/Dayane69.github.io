Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Who we are

autok is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by Dayane. It has no users other than the operator.

Data we access

When the operator connects their own TikTok account, the app receives, with the operator's consent:

  • basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) to identify the connected account;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, used only to upload and publish videos on that account;
  • the list and status of videos published through the app, to confirm publication.

How data is stored and used

All data is stored on the operator's own computer. Tokens are stored encrypted. Data is used only to publish the operator's own videos and is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data about other TikTok users is collected.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account is connected. Disconnecting the account in the app, or revoking access in TikTok's settings, removes the app's access; stored tokens are then deleted.
